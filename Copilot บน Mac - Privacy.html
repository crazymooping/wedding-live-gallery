Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Wedding Live Gallery is a small personal tool used by a photographer to show wedding photos to guests in real time. It has two parts: (1) a desktop uploader script that runs on the photographer's own computer and (2) this public gallery web page.

What the uploader accesses

The uploader asks for the Google Drive scope drive.file and uses it only to upload photos and videos from a folder on the photographer's computer into one Google Drive folder owned by the photographer. It does not read, list, change or delete any other files in the Google account. It is used only by the app owner; no other users sign in to it.

What this web page does

The gallery page displays the photos in that one shared Drive folder. Guests are not asked to sign in and the page does not collect names, emails or any other personal information. A guest's "favorite" (heart) selections are stored only in that guest's own browser (localStorage) and are never sent to us.

Use and sharing of Google user data

Google user data is used solely to upload the event photos to the owner's Drive folder. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Photos are stored in the photographer's own Google Drive and can be deleted by the photographer at any time. Authorization tokens are kept only on the photographer's computer and can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Photos of guests

If you appear in a photo and would like it removed from the gallery, please contact the photographer using the email below and it will be deleted.
